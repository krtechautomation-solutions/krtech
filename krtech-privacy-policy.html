Privacy Policy

KRTech Machine Alerts and WhatsApp business communications

Effective date: 9 November 2026 · Service provider: KR Tech (KRTech Machine Alerts), Tamil Nadu, India

This policy explains how KR Tech handles personal information in connection with its industrial monitoring, customer-support and WhatsApp machine-alert services (collectively, the “Services”). It covers interactions with our WhatsApp Business account, the KRTech messaging gateway, and related customer interfaces. This policy should be read alongside any separate contract or service terms applicable to a customer installation.

1. Information we may collect

  • Contact and account details: names, business names, telephone and WhatsApp numbers, email addresses, and customer or device identifiers supplied in connection with the Services.
  • Communications: WhatsApp messages sent to and from our business account, message metadata, delivery/read/error statuses, and customer-support requests.
  • Machine and operational data: device names or identifiers, machine states, alarms, fault descriptions, timestamps, and other technical telemetry configured by customers for notification services.
  • Security and technical records: authentication events, API request identifiers, error logs, and information needed to operate, troubleshoot and protect the Services.

2. How we use the information

We use information to configure and operate customer services; send requested machine-status, fault and service notifications; respond to customer inquiries; confirm message delivery and troubleshoot failures; maintain service security and reliability; and satisfy applicable legal obligations. We do not use customer machine alerts to make unrelated marketing claims.

3. WhatsApp and service providers

When we communicate through WhatsApp, message and account data is processed through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform according to Meta’s applicable terms and privacy practices. Technical infrastructure providers may also process information on our behalf for hosting, networking, storage and service operation. Information may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect rights and system security.

4. Consent and notification preferences

Customers should authorize the WhatsApp numbers and recipients used for operational notifications. Where required, we obtain opt-in before initiating business messages, and we respect applicable messaging rules, including WhatsApp’s conversation-window and template requirements. To request a change to alert recipients or stop notifications, contact us using the details below. Stopping alerts may affect the operation of the requested notification service.

5. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the Services, customer support, security, contractual obligations, or applicable law. Retention periods can differ by data type and customer arrangement. We use access controls and other reasonable safeguards, but no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your privacy requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with our Services, subject to applicable legal, contractual and security requirements. Include enough information for us to identify your account or communication, but do not send passwords or access tokens by email. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

7. International processing

Our use of Meta and other technical service providers may involve processing information in jurisdictions outside your own. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the relevant providers’ terms and required legal safeguards.

8. Children’s data

The Services are intended for business and industrial use, not for children. We do not knowingly design the Services to collect children’s personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our Services or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
